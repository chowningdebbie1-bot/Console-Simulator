Technology Museum
The simulator can move through several generations of interface design.

1980s

Firmware screens, text interfaces, simple startup diagnostics and monochrome displays.

1990s

Classic desktop interfaces, CRT-style displays, boot menus and early graphical operating systems.

2000s

Windows XP-era desktops, classic Mac interfaces, sixth-generation consoles and early smartphones.

2010s

Touch interfaces, tile-based desktop interfaces, modern console dashboards and app stores.

2020s

Modern desktop, tablet, phone and console interfaces with translucent panels and dynamic layouts.

2026 simulator era

All of the simulated generations are connected through one universal device laboratory.
